Context Inspector prototype

Coding-assistant chat with a simulated assistant. Nothing is sent over the network.

Walkthrough (about 2 minutes)
  1. Press Use next scripted message then Send, five times. Message 1 sets a rule: never modify my architecture, fix bugs only.
  2. Reply 5 proposes a rewrite. The chat gives no sign that the rule was lost.
  3. Open the Inspector (right). Turns 1–3 were replaced by a system summary, and your rule sentence is flagged as missing.
  4. Fix it: Show originals, then Pin the flagged sentence (or Restore originals).
  5. Press Re-answer. Compare v1 and v2 on the reply. Undo reverses any Inspector action.
  6. Try the other condition: switch the Inspector off and repeat. Your only fix is to retype the rule.
